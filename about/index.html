About Alek Koldal, EA

Koldal Tax & Advisory helps small business owners keep accurate books, make informed decisions, and stop dreading tax season.

I'm an IRS Enrolled Agent, licensed by the U.S. Department of the Treasury to prepare returns and represent taxpayers before the IRS. Day to day I work mainly in QuickBooks Online, handling monthly bookkeeping, cleanups and catch-ups, so your books and your taxes are handled by the same person.

Based in Coral Springs, Florida, I believe responsive communication, personalized service, and practical solutions are the foundation of every good client relationship. My goal is to be more than your bookkeeper: a trusted advisor and long-term partner in your business.
