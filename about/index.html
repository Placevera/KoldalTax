About Alek Koldal, EA

Koldal Tax & Advisory helps small business owners keep accurate books, make informed decisions, and stop dreading tax season.

I'm an IRS Enrolled Agent, licensed by the U.S. Department of the Treasury to prepare returns and represent taxpayers before the IRS. My days are spent on tax returns, planning and the complicated problems: IRS notices, unfiled years and penalties.

Our bookkeeping team keeps clients' QuickBooks Online books current every month, so the books are kept with the tax return in mind and an Enrolled Agent is in the same firm when questions come up. Based in Coral Springs, Florida, we believe in responsive communication and practical answers.

The practice carries professional liability (errors & omissions), cyber liability and general liability insurance, so your finances and your data are protected.
