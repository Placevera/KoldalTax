Payroll with Gusto, set up and checked

Payroll runs through Gusto, which files payroll taxes and W-2s. I set it up right, connect it to QuickBooks Online, and review every payroll so your books and your payroll match. Based in Coral Springs and working with clients in Margate, Parkland, Coconut Creek, Tamarac and Pompano Beach, as well as remotely across Florida.

What I do

  • Set up your Gusto account, employees and contractors
  • Connect Gusto to QuickBooks Online with the right accounts
  • Review each payroll as it posts to your books
  • Make sure owner pay is handled correctly for S corporations
  • Reconcile payroll liabilities at year end

Contractors

Paying contractors through Gusto? I make sure their W-9s are collected and their 1099s are right.
