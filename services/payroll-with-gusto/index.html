Payroll with Gusto, set up and checked

Payroll runs through Gusto, which files payroll taxes and W-2s. We set it up right, connect it to QuickBooks Online, and review every payroll so your books and your payroll match. Based in Coral Springs and working with clients in Margate, Parkland, Coconut Creek, Tamarac and Pompano Beach, as well as remotely across Florida.

What we do

  • Set up your Gusto account, employees and contractors
  • Connect Gusto to QuickBooks Online with the right accounts
  • Review each payroll as it posts to your books
  • Make sure owner pay is handled correctly for S corporations
  • Reconcile payroll liabilities at year end

Contractors

For bookkeeping clients, we track contractor payments in QuickBooks during the year and prepare the 1099s for your review each January. You confirm the amounts, and we file them.
