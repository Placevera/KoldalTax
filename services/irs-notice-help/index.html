Services

Help with IRS notices and letters

An IRS letter doesn't have to be stressful. As an Enrolled Agent, I'm federally licensed to represent taxpayers before the IRS. Based in Coral Springs and working with clients in Margate, Parkland, Coconut Creek, Tamarac and Pompano Beach, as well as remotely across Florida.

What I can do

  • Explain what your notice means and what it will cost
  • Respond to proposed changes such as a CP2000
  • Set up payment plans for balances due
  • Respond to identity verification and missing information letters
  • Protect your appeal rights when time limits apply

Penalty relief

Many penalties can be reduced or removed, through first-time abatement or reasonable cause. I charge a small upfront fee plus a percentage of the penalties the IRS actually removes, so you mostly pay when it works. The first penalty review is free.

What to send me

A copy of every page of the notice, and the tax return for that year if you have it.
