Services

QuickBooks cleanup & catch-up

Get caught up, then stay caught up. We bring your QuickBooks Online file current, reconcile every account, and leave you with books ready for your tax return. Based in Coral Springs and working with clients in Margate, Parkland, Coconut Creek, Tamarac and Pompano Beach, as well as remotely across Florida.

What's included

  • Prior months cleaned up and every bank and credit card account reconciled
  • Uncategorized and Ask My Accountant transactions sorted
  • Duplicates, Undeposited Funds and opening balances fixed
  • Bank rules set up so transactions categorize correctly
  • Your books tied to your last tax return

How pricing works

It's a one-time fee, usually $800 to $2,500 depending on how much cleanup your file needs. We quote the exact amount after looking at your file, before any work starts. After that, you move onto a monthly plan so it never piles up again.

Taxes behind too?

As an Enrolled Agent I can also file missing returns and deal with any IRS notices that came while you were catching up.
