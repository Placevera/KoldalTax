Services

QuickBooks cleanup & catch-up

Get caught up, then stay caught up. I bring your QuickBooks Online file current, reconcile every account, and leave you with books ready for your tax return. Based in Coral Springs and working with clients in Margate, Parkland, Coconut Creek, Tamarac and Pompano Beach, as well as remotely across Florida.

What's included

  • Bank and credit card accounts reconciled month by month
  • Uncategorized and Ask My Accountant transactions sorted
  • Duplicates, Undeposited Funds and opening balances fixed
  • Loan and owner draw entries corrected
  • A clean profit & loss and balance sheet at the end

How pricing works

After a free review of your file I quote a fixed price for the cleanup. When it's done, you can move onto a monthly plan so it never piles up again.

Taxes behind too?

As an Enrolled Agent I can also file missing returns and deal with any IRS notices that came while you were catching up.
