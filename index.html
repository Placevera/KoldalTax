Enrolled Agent

Your bookkeeper is also a federally licensed tax professional

Enrolled Agents are licensed by the U.S. Department of the Treasury and have unlimited rights to represent taxpayers before the IRS, for any tax matter, in all 50 states. It's the highest credential the IRS awards.

That means your books are kept with your tax return in mind, and if the IRS ever sends a letter, the person who knows your numbers is the one who can answer it.
