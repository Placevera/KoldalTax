Why an Enrolled Agent

The highest credential the IRS awards

Enrolled Agents are licensed by the U.S. Department of the Treasury and can represent taxpayers before the IRS on any tax matter, in every state.

Working with one person for your books, returns and planning means nothing gets lost between advisors, and if the IRS ever writes, the person who knows your numbers is the one who answers.
