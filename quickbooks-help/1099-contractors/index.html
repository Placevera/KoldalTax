Preparing 1099s in QuickBooks Online

Why it happens

If you paid an unincorporated contractor $600 or more for services during the year, you generally need to issue a Form 1099-NEC. QuickBooks can prepare them, but only if vendors and accounts are set up correctly.

How to fix it

  1. Collect a Form W-9 from each contractor before paying them
  2. Mark those vendors as 1099 eligible and enter their tax ID
  3. Map your contractor expense accounts in the 1099 setup
  4. Review the 1099 report, then file and send forms by January 31
  5. Payments made by credit card or through apps like PayPal are generally excluded, since those processors report them

When to get help

Missing W-9s and wrong tax IDs are the usual problem. Late or incorrect 1099s can bring IRS penalties.
