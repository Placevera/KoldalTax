Personal expenses paid from the business account

Why it happens

Personal spending isn't a business expense. Recording it as one overstates your deductions, which is a common issue the IRS looks for.

How to fix it

  1. Create an equity account for owner draws (sole proprietor or LLC) or shareholder distributions (S corporation)
  2. Categorize personal purchases to that equity account, not to an expense
  3. Set up a bank rule if the same personal charges repeat
  4. Better still, keep a separate personal card for personal spending

When to get help

For S corporations, distributions and owner pay have tax consequences, so it's worth reviewing them with your tax preparer.
