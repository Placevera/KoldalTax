Months behind on bookkeeping

Why it happens

It happens to most small business owners: the bank feed piles up, receipts get lost, and tax time arrives with books that aren't ready.

How to fix it

  1. Download statements for every bank and credit card account for the missing months
  2. Make sure each account's starting balance matches the first statement
  3. Work through the bank feed month by month, using bank rules for repeat transactions
  4. Reconcile each month as you finish it
  5. Review the profit and loss for anything that looks off before filing taxes
