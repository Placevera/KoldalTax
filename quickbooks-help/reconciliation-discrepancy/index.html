Reconciliation doesn't match your bank statement

Why it happens

A transaction from an already-reconciled period was edited, deleted or added after you reconciled, so the beginning balance no longer matches the statement. It can also come from a transaction entered twice or a deposit recorded for the wrong amount.

How to fix it

  1. Check that the beginning balance on the reconcile screen equals your statement's beginning balance
  2. Open the reconciliation history for the account and view the report for the last good period to see changes made after reconciling
  3. Look for duplicates, deleted transactions and amounts that are off by a transposed digit
  4. Fix the changed transactions rather than forcing the difference with an adjustment

When to get help

If several months don't tie out, an accountant can undo and redo reconciliations from QuickBooks Online Accountant without losing your work.
