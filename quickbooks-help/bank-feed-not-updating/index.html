QuickBooks Online bank feed not updating

Why it happens

The connection between QuickBooks and your bank broke. Common causes are a changed bank password, a new security or multi-factor step, the bank changing how it connects, or an account that was closed or renumbered.

How to fix it

  1. Go to Transactions (or Banking) and click the refresh or Update button for the account
  2. If you see an error, sign in again with your current bank login and complete any security prompts
  3. Check your bank's website for a notice about connection changes
  4. If it still won't connect, download a .QBO or .CSV file from your bank and upload it for the missing dates
  5. Avoid disconnecting and reconnecting with an earlier start date, which can pull in duplicates

When to get help

If transactions are missing for months, or you reconnected and now have duplicates, it's usually faster to have the account cleaned up and reconciled in one pass.
