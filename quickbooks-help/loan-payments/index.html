Loan payments recorded as an expense

Why it happens

Only the interest part of a loan payment is an expense. The principal part reduces the loan balance. If the whole payment is categorized as an expense, your profit is understated and the loan balance on your balance sheet is wrong.

How to fix it

  1. Set up the loan as a liability account
  2. Split each payment: principal to the loan account, interest to an interest expense account
  3. Use your lender's statement or amortization schedule for the split
  4. Reconcile the loan account to the lender's statement at least yearly

When to get help

Vehicle and equipment loans also affect depreciation, so it's worth getting these right before tax time.
