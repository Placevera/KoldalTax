Payments to deposit (Undeposited Funds) balance is too high

Why it happens

Customer payments were recorded against invoices but never grouped into a bank deposit. Then the real deposit from the bank feed was added as new income, so income is counted twice and the Payments to deposit account keeps growing.

How to fix it

  1. Open the Payments to deposit (Undeposited Funds) register to see the old payments
  2. For each bank deposit, create a Bank Deposit that groups the matching customer payments
  3. In the bank feed, match the deposit to that Bank Deposit instead of adding it as income
  4. Find deposits already added as income and change them to match the payments

When to get help

A large old balance usually means income is overstated, which means you may be overpaying tax. It's worth fixing before you file.
