Common questions

What counts as a transaction?

Each line in your bank or credit card feed: a deposit, a purchase, a transfer. Your free review gives me the real count.

Do I need my own QuickBooks Online subscription?

Yes. You own your file and your data. If you don't have QuickBooks Online yet, I'll help you pick the right version and set it up.

I'm behind. Can I still start?

Yes. We start with a cleanup and catch-up for a quoted price, then move you onto a monthly plan.

Do you run payroll?

Payroll runs through Gusto. I set up your Gusto account, connect it to QuickBooks, and review every payroll so it's recorded correctly.

Can you do my taxes too?

Yes. I'm an IRS Enrolled Agent. Your business return is included in Advisory and discounted on the other plans, and I prepare individual returns starting at $650.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Plans are month to month.
