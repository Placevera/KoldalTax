IRS CP268: We changed your business return: you have a credit

Low: informational For: Businesses

What this notice means

The IRS corrected an error on your business return, and the change gives you an overpayment. It is not a bill.

Common reasons you got it

  • A math or calculation error on the return
  • Payments or credits not counted correctly

What to do

  • Compare the change to your return
  • Within 30 days, call to ask for a refund check or to apply the credit to other taxes
  • Contact the IRS if you disagree

Deadline

Call within 30 days if you want to direct how the credit is used.

Get help with your CP268

As an IRS Enrolled Agent, I can review your notice, explain what it means for you, and respond to the IRS on your behalf with a signed Form 2848 (Power of Attorney). Send your details and I'll get back to you.
