IRS Letter 12C: We need more information to process your return

Medium: action needed For: Individuals

What this notice means

Your return is missing information or forms, so the IRS can't finish processing it.

Common reasons you got it

  • Missing Form 8962 for Marketplace health insurance (Form 1095-A)
  • Missing W-2 or withholding information
  • Missing schedules

What to do

  • Send the requested form or information
  • Include a copy of the letter with your response

Deadline

Respond by the date in the letter, usually 20 days, or your refund may be reduced.

Get help with your Letter 12C

As an IRS Enrolled Agent, I can review your notice, explain what it means for you, and respond to the IRS on your behalf. Send your details and I'll get back to you.

This is general information, not tax advice for your situation. Always go by the dates and instructions printed on your own notice. Official IRS explanation: Understanding your IRS notice or letter.
