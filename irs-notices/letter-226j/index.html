IRS Letter 226J: Proposed health coverage penalty for employers

Urgent: time-sensitive rights For: Employers

What this notice means

The IRS is proposing an Affordable Care Act penalty (the employer shared responsibility payment) because it believes your business, as an employer with 50 or more full-time workers, didn't offer qualifying health coverage. It is a proposal, not a bill.

Common reasons you got it

  • A full-time employee got a premium tax credit on the Marketplace
  • Errors in the codes on Forms 1095-C
  • Coverage that didn't meet affordability or minimum value rules

What to do

  • Review your Forms 1095-C and the employee list the IRS sent
  • Respond with Form 14764, and Form 14765 to correct any employee details
  • Explain any coverage offers or safe harbors that apply
  • If you disagree after the response, you can ask for an Appeals review

Deadline

Respond by the date in the letter, at least 90 days for letters issued from 2025. If you don't, the IRS assesses the penalty (CP220J).

Get help with your Letter 226J

As an IRS Enrolled Agent, I can review your notice, explain what it means for you, and respond to the IRS on your behalf with a signed Form 2848 (Power of Attorney). Send your details and I'll get back to you.

This is general information, not tax advice for your situation. Always go by the dates and instructions printed on your own notice. Official IRS explanation: Understanding your IRS notice or letter.
