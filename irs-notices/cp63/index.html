IRS CP63: Your refund is on hold until you file missing returns

Response required For: Individuals

Always check with the IRS. IRS notices and rules change, and this page may be out of date. Go by the dates and instructions printed on your own notice, and confirm on IRS.gov.

What this notice means

The IRS is holding your refund because one or more earlier returns haven't been filed and it believes you'll owe tax on them. It is not a bill.

Common reasons you got it

  • A return from a prior year was never filed
  • A return was filed but not processed or was lost

What to do

  • File the missing returns right away
  • If you already filed or didn't need to, explain that in your reply
  • Keep copies of everything you send

Deadline

Respond by the date on the notice. If you don't, the IRS may prepare a return for you (CP2566), usually with a higher tax.

Look up CP63 on IRS.gov
