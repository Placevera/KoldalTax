IRS CP05: We're reviewing your return

Low: informational For: Individuals

What this notice means

The IRS is reviewing your return to verify income, withholding, credits or business income before issuing your refund. It does not mean you did something wrong.

Common reasons you got it

  • Random or automated verification of withholding and credits

What to do

  • No action is needed unless the IRS asks for more information
  • Keep your W-2s, 1099s and records handy
  • Wait for the follow-up notice

Deadline

No response required. Review typically takes several weeks.
