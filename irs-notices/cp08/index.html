IRS CP08: You may qualify for the Additional Child Tax Credit

Review for accuracy For: Individuals

Always check with the IRS. IRS notices and rules change, and this page may be out of date. Go by the dates and instructions printed on your own notice, and confirm on IRS.gov.

What this notice means

IRS records show you may be able to claim the Additional Child Tax Credit, the refundable part of the Child Tax Credit, but you didn't. It is not a bill; it's a chance at more refund.

Common reasons you got it

  • You have a qualifying child but didn't claim the credit
  • The credit was left off when the return was prepared

What to do

  • Complete the Form 15110 worksheet included with the notice
  • If you have at least one qualifying child, send Form 15110 and Schedule 8812
  • Send it through the IRS Document Upload Tool or by mail, as the notice explains

Deadline

Optional. Respond by the date on the notice if you want the credit.

Look up CP08 on IRS.gov
