IRS LT11 / Letter 1058: Final notice of intent to levy and your right to a hearing

Urgent: time-sensitive rights For: Individuals and businesses

What this notice means

The IRS's final notice before it can levy wages, bank accounts and other assets. It also gives you the right to a Collection Due Process (CDP) hearing with the IRS Independent Office of Appeals.

Common reasons you got it

  • Unpaid balances after earlier notices

What to do

  • Pay in full or arrange a payment plan
  • To protect your appeal rights, file Form 12153 (Request for a Collection Due Process hearing) on time
  • Talk to an Enrolled Agent or other tax professional now

Deadline

You have 30 days from the notice date to request a CDP hearing. Missing it limits your options.
