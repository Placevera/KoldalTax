IRS Letter 525: Proposed audit changes

High: respond by the deadline For: Individuals

What this notice means

After examining your return, the IRS is proposing changes, usually additional tax. You can agree, or disagree and provide more information or request an appeal.

Common reasons you got it

  • Documents sent during an audit weren't enough
  • No response to an earlier audit letter

What to do

  • Review the examination report
  • Sign the agreement form if you agree
  • If you disagree, send more information or request an Appeals conference

Deadline

Usually 30 days from the letter date. After that, a Statutory Notice of Deficiency may follow.
