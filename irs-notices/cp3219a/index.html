IRS CP3219A / Letter 3219: Statutory Notice of Deficiency

Urgent: time-sensitive rights For: Individuals

What this notice means

The IRS has determined you owe additional tax and is formally telling you of your right to challenge it in U.S. Tax Court. Often follows an unanswered CP2000 or audit.

Common reasons you got it

  • No response to a CP2000
  • Audit changes you didn't agree to

What to do

  • Decide whether you agree with the changes
  • If you disagree, file a petition with the U.S. Tax Court before the deadline
  • Don't ignore it: once the deadline passes the tax is assessed

Deadline

90 days from the notice date to petition Tax Court (150 days if addressed to you outside the U.S.). The deadline cannot be extended.
