IRS CP504: Notice of intent to levy

High: respond by the deadline For: Individuals

What this notice means

The IRS intends to seize (levy) your state tax refund to pay the balance, and warns it may levy other property or rights. It can also lead to a federal tax lien.

Common reasons you got it

  • Balance unpaid after CP14, CP501 and CP503

What to do

  • Pay in full, or set up a payment plan right away
  • If you can't pay, ask about hardship status or other options
  • Get professional help before a final levy notice arrives

Deadline

Act by the date on the notice. The next step is usually a final notice with appeal rights (LT11 or Letter 1058).
