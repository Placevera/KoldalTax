IRS Letter 950: 30-day letter: proposed audit changes

Urgent: time-sensitive rights For: Individuals and businesses

What this notice means

After a field audit you didn't agree with, the IRS is proposing changes, usually more tax and possibly penalties. You have 30 days to take the case to the IRS Independent Office of Appeals. It is a proposal, not a bill.

Common reasons you got it

  • Disagreement with the audit findings
  • Documents that didn't support deductions or income

What to do

  • Read the audit report carefully
  • File a written protest within 30 days to go to Appeals
  • Ask whether any accuracy penalty can be removed for reasonable cause
  • If you agree, sign the agreement form

Deadline

30 days from the letter date to file a protest. Otherwise a Statutory Notice of Deficiency follows.
