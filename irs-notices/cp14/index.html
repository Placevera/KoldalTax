IRS CP14: You have a balance due

Medium: action needed For: Individuals

What this notice means

The first bill for unpaid tax. It shows the tax, penalties and interest owed as of the notice date. Interest and late-payment penalties keep growing until it's paid.

Common reasons you got it

  • Return filed without full payment
  • Estimated payments missed or applied to the wrong year
  • A change the IRS made after processing your return

What to do

  • Check that the balance is right, including any payments you made
  • Pay in full by the due date, or apply for a payment plan
  • If you disagree, contact the IRS at the number on the notice

Deadline

Pay by the date on the notice (usually 21 days) to limit extra interest and penalties.
