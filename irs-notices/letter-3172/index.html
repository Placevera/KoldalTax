IRS Letter 3172: A tax lien was filed: your right to a hearing

Urgent: time-sensitive rights For: Individuals and businesses

Always check with the IRS. IRS notices and rules change, and this page may be out of date. Go by the dates and instructions printed on your own notice, and confirm on IRS.gov.

What this notice means

The IRS filed a Notice of Federal Tax Lien, a public claim against your property for unpaid tax, and is telling you of your right to a hearing. It relates to the balance you already owe.

Common reasons you got it

  • Unpaid balance after earlier notices
  • No payment plan in place

What to do

  • Request a Collection Due Process hearing with Form 12153 by the date in the letter
  • Ask about lien withdrawal, discharge or subordination (options that remove or limit the lien)
  • Pay in full or set up a payment plan

Deadline

Request a hearing by the date in the letter, about 30 days. After that, the lien stays and levies can follow.

Look up Letter 3172 on IRS.gov
