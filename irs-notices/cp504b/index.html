IRS CP504B: Notice of intent to levy: business

Response required For: Businesses

Always check with the IRS. IRS notices and rules change, and this page may be out of date. Go by the dates and instructions printed on your own notice, and confirm on IRS.gov.

What this notice means

Your business has an unpaid balance and the IRS intends to take (levy) its property or rights to property, and may file a federal tax lien. This is a bill.

Common reasons you got it

  • Business balance unpaid after earlier notices
  • Payroll or other business taxes not paid

What to do

  • Pay right away, or arrange a payment plan, an offer in compromise (a settlement for less) or hardship status
  • Call the IRS if you disagree; you can also request a Collection Appeals Program review
  • Check whether you've already received a final notice with hearing rights (LT11 or Letter 1058)
  • Ask about penalty relief (reasonable cause, or first-time abatement if it wasn't applied automatically)

Deadline

Act immediately. The next step is a final notice with hearing rights (LT11 or Letter 1058), then levy.
